## Settings and templates

- [Typed starting preferences](profiles/payton-defaults.json) mirror selected profile settings; they do not override your edited profile.
- [Tight teaching reference](profiles/TIGHT%20TEACHING%20REFERENCE.md) separates measured source observations from candidate settings.
- [Caption calibration](profiles/caption-calibration.json) retains the actual historical reference and its qualifications.
- [Edit brief](templates/EDIT%20BRIEF.md), [source and review](templates/SOURCE%20AND%20REVIEW.md), and [delivery and learning](templates/DELIVERY%20AND%20LEARNING.md) keep the required decisions in three reusable records.
- [Synthetic example](examples/README.md) and [fixture generator](examples/make_fixture.py) support a disposable route test.

The `presets` folder includes the native keyboard map, sanitized layout, three native export-setting files and the owned shot-fitted DCTL finishing transform. Back up your current settings before selecting an optional import. A preset name does not prove a correct source frame rate, media location or camera transform.

The [tools guide](references/TOOLS.md) lists all17 maintained helper modules and their dependencies. Optional renderers and styles live in [motion](../motion/START%20HERE.md). Setup and customization-preserving installation live in [setup](../setup/START%20HERE.md). The complete base pack remains beside this system for the editor playbooks and shared interchange exporter.

Read [source and rights](SOURCE%20AND%20RIGHTS.md) before redistributing third-party additions.
