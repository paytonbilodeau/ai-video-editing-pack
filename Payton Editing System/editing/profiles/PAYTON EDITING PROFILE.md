# Payton-derived editing profile

This is a filled-in starting point, adapted from Payton's working system. Copy it into your own settings folder once; keep your existing profile instead when you already have one. Change only what matters to your work. These are preferences and procedures, not a guarantee that every AI/editor can execute them.

## The starting taste

**Pace:** Tight, natural teaching. Start on the first complete useful word, keep the hook and payoff in order, remove recording housekeeping and verified mistakes, then refine the small spaces inside phrases and at joins. Protect quiet consonants, useful breaths, reading time, demonstrations and intentional emphasis. Do not impose a global silence length or a target cut count.

**Takes and mistakes:** Compare whole thoughts. Inspect the latest clean complete take first, but prefer an earlier take when it is more accurate, clear or natural. Remove abandoned starts and redundant failed attempts only after hearing them in context. Similar wording, a waveform dip or missing ASR text is not enough. If the speaker uses “cut cut” or “full stop restart” as real recording commands, verify their meaning before using them; quoted commands in a lesson are not restart instructions.

**Picture:** Correct exposure, white balance and shot matching before adding a look. Keep skin, highlights and authentic screen colors credible. Use one verified source-to-display transform. Default to a restrained natural grade. A warm filmic interpretation is an optional branch for suitable camera footage, not a universal LUT or tint over interfaces.

**Sound:** Keep the real voice recognizable. Diagnose the microphone, channel mapping and defect first. Payton's preferred full-edit finish uses mic-matched Auphonic once after picture completion. This portable default is a preference to offer, not authorization to use your account or upload footage. Keep a working native route when chosen; do not stack two complete enhancement chains. Review drafts for a creator's cut pass stay pre-Auphonic.

**Graphics:** Explain a relationship, action or change that the viewer needs. Keep the presenter as the narrative spine, reveal one focus at a time and return to useful source footage after the graphic finishes its job. Preserve ordinary landscape framing; do not add constant zooms or decorative motion. The sibling motion component carries the complete visual styles and their editable implementations.

**Captions:** Use short natural phrases with accurate names and real speech timing. Default to readable white text with restrained emphasis, a soft dark shadow and a subtle stroke when needed. Keep eyes, mouth, controls, pointers and results clear. For a creator finishing captions in CapCut, deliver a clean master and editable sidecars instead of burning them in. Assistant-made phrase captions are the alternate route, not a compulsory second caption layer.

**Formats:** Keep the source's exact frame rate. Prefer 3840×2160 landscape or 2160×3840 portrait H.264/AAC when the source/project and brief support 4K; 4K enlargement does not recover missing detail. Keep 48 kHz audio and progressive-download optimization. Use a high-quality intermediate when another editor or alpha layer needs it. Compose portrait separately rather than cropping blindly.

**Portrait teaching:** Start from a readable screen demonstration above a substantial presenter panel when both matter. Use full-camera portrait for solo speech and a separate paired layout for comparisons. Fit the actual action and platform interface; do not make a tiny screen fit inside a fixed decorative frame. For the inherited 4K portrait phrase-caption treatment, 92 px type inside a 1640 px column is a reference starting point, scaled with the canvas and checked on the actual phone-size image. It is not a universal safe-zone guarantee.

**Review:** Finish the clean cut and actual source repairs before a creator's taste pass. Preserve the baseline, let the creator edit the duplicate undisturbed, then inspect the exact returned project. Lock approved timing before visuals. Keep source footage, editable project and final export distinct. Do not call a technically valid render fully reviewed unless the stated audiovisual review happened.

## Consequential choices to confirm once

Reuse answers already known. Ask only when relevant: your editor and working folder; landscape/portrait deliverable; natural versus stronger filmic look; actual microphone; native versus optional cloud audio; who adds captions; and whether a clean-cut review is wanted before graphics. Keep current project exceptions in its brief.

## Scope of the numbers

The native mic amounts, EQ search ranges and -16 LUFS / ≤-1 dBTP encoded target in the audio references are Payton-derived audition/comparison defaults. Another delivery brief can override them. They are not universal platform rules or certified equivalents to a provider preset. Exact private project gains, fixed face masks and old pause thresholds are not carried forward as universal instructions.

The optional [tighter teaching reference](TIGHT%20TEACHING%20REFERENCE.md) retains the actual measured pacing examples and their limits. [Advanced handoffs](../references/COMPARISONS%20AND%20ADVANCED%20HANDOFFS.md) covers paired demos, split production and the separate-stem/CapCut branch.
