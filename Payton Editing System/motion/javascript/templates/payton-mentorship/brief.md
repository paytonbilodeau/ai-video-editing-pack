# Time, frame, motion

Private silent setup proof, 9 seconds at 30 fps. Three readable states show
the mechanism of procedural animation: select time, draw a frame, then play
frames in order. The circle is a schematic object, not product evidence.

This is a transparent starter for an opaque Flow board or an explicitly chosen
overlay. Large white labels and mint moving objects use AM colors. No public
creator's words, drawings or identity are reproduced. The empty subtitle lane
belongs to the finished composition. Use an actual presenter composite before
accepting a future talking-head overlay.

For landscape, update both piece.json format and the W/H constants in head.html;
the renderer lays the diagram inside a centered landscape region. Metadata
checks must pass after any FPS or duration change. Replace this demonstration
story and timing with the actual locked edit before production.

No narration, music, SFX, paid generation, Auphonic call or publication is part
of this test. Technical validation and visual self-review are separate from
Payton accepting the design on a real video.
