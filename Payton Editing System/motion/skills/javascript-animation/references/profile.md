# Payton's JavaScript motion profile

Requested October 6, 2026: add procedural JavaScript animation to the portable
editing system, tailored to Tech Motion short-form,
the AM colorway, and established Remotion use. This is a Payton-derived routing and
starting template, not a new claim that Payton has approved a finished design.

Read the Flow skill and the customer editing profile for semantic timing, caption ownership and music choices. A procedural renderer does not justify changing an accepted style.

Use **Tech Motion** as the visual-style name. Tech Explainer, Digital Motion
and Digital Explainer are equivalent aliases. Flow is the internal Remotion
toolkit. The [naming map](../../../START%20HERE.md)
keeps tools, styles, colorways and workflows separate.

## Color and composition

Use black/white and neutral gray as the base. Dark AI Mentorship scenes use
mint `#72DFA5` for the readable point of attention and Old Money Green
`#0F583D` for depth. The deeper green is not small body text on black.
Light treatment reverses emphasis to Old Money Green on off-white. Keep the
green restrained; no automatic neon treatment or generic blue.

At 1080 by 1920, use the current house starting guide: primary diagram roughly
inside x145..915/y380..1220 and reserve x180..900/y1300..1490 for subtitles.
Those are house composition guides, not guaranteed platform safe areas.
Inspect the actual final composite and current destination preview. Recompose
landscape and square scenes rather than cropping the portrait output.

Use the established local typography when the project loads it. The included
small test template uses a heavy system sans-serif fallback so its renderer
test can run without downloading a font. It does not replace Flow's approved
font system. No debug labels, scene IDs, registration marks or ornamental
interface elements belong in an audience-facing export.

## Movement that explains the sentence

Start with the causal object: what enters, what changes, and what leaves.
Prefer an existing object transforming through the explanation over unrelated
cards for every noun. Keep its position and velocity continuous at handoffs;
use a meaningful settled hold rather than a last-frame snap. Avoid overlapping
old and new labels during scene changes. Sample actual exported frames around
each handoff and review the whole motion at delivery scale. JavaScript is useful for drawn paths, repeatable particle
or geometry systems, visualized proportions, diagrams and custom masks.
Do not infer scientific outcomes from decorative animation.

Keep Flow's designed, evidence, physical and hybrid choices purposeful. Real
evidence needs a genuine artifact; physics needs enough time to settle and a
fixed-step bake. Keep the hook, meaningful pivot and verdict on Payton when
the actual passage calls for them. No arbitrary graphic quota is added here.

Entrance, hold and exit belong to the spoken thought. Cue the first change on
the verified matching word, let fast lists accumulate, and hold the complete
state long enough to read. Do not make labels drift while they are being read.
Use typewriter motion only for typing or a deliberate writing beat. The
Gentlemen signature keeps its own skill, typography and pen-event sound route;
do not substitute Animate's sketchbook style for it or for Payton's Sketch.

## Remotion and sound

Flow owns the master clock. The frame-acknowledged JavaScript component must
use the local Sequence frame divided by composition FPS. Verify at scene
boundaries and non-sequential seeks. The HTML must finish drawing the requested
frame before Remotion captures it. Exported clips also need an explicit start,
duration and FPS mapping. Never run a free-playing iframe in a render.

Stock Animate styles are generally opaque paper/board worlds. Keep them opaque
inside an intentional beat. The supplied transparent template clears every
frame and only draws its objects. PNG or ProRes 4444 preserves that alpha;
H.264 MP4 does not. Decode an exported frame and composite over a contrasting
background before relying on the transparency in an editor.

No ElevenLabs subscription or connector is required. Use the customer's recorded
voice and verified timing by default. Optional local synthesis can make a
scratch timing reference, clearly labeled. New paid narration, generated music
or uploads need their own authority. Preserve chosen music and derive effects
from surviving visual events. Do not import upstream loudness/score formulas
as a replacement for the accepted personal mix and one-pass final audio route.
