# Portable SFX replacements

These files are newly rendered from the original Payton Flow procedural SFX engine, included in `motion/tools/flow_sfx.py`. They replace the upstream Pixabay source recordings, which are not distributed in this bundle. They are synthetic UI cues, not recordings and not the original upstream timbres. Filenames preserve the template interface. Audition and balance them with your own narration; a matching name does not prove the right creative fit. The Gentlemen selected sound uses its separate credited CC0 sources.
