# Turn AI into your video editor

Download, unzip, and give the whole **Vibe Editors** folder to Codex or Claude
Code on your computer. Paste the message below. Your AI handles the setup and
explains the choices that matter. No recording is needed yet.

Already have an editor, skills or a workflow? Keep them. The pack should connect
to what you use and fill the gaps. The whole free system is here, including the
tools and teaching guide. You don't need to wait for the emails.

```text
Set up this Vibe Editors folder so you can be my video editor. Read its START HERE guide and follow the setup skill. Ask how hands-on I want to be: careful, balanced, or handle it for me. Reuse my existing tools, permissions, skills and preferences. Keep my originals and projects safe, and keep my private editing profile outside the pack. Explain any new access, upload, installation or cost before using it unless already authorized. Offer useful optional tools, then set up what fits. Verify what works and tell me the simplest next step. No footage is needed for setup.
```

## What happens next

Your AI checks the tools it can already use, helps connect your editor, and
saves your preferences privately. If something needs your click, it explains
the exact choice and why it matters. It should distinguish a tested connection
from a route it has only read about.

Then give it a recording and say what you want: a clean cut, a complete video,
motion graphics, or clips from a longer recording. It uses the parts this job
needs. You review the result and keep creative control.
