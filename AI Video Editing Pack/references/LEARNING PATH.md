You do not need to finish another course before using the pack. Start with your
editor's official guide, then choose a tutorial for the technique you need.

## Official help for your editor

| Editor | Resource | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DaVinci Resolve | [Blackmagic Design training](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/training) | Editing, color, Fairlight audio and Fusion lessons, with training books and project files. Choose the subject you need. |
| Premiere | [Adobe Learn](https://www.adobe.com/learn/premiere-pro) | Adobe's tutorial library. Match the demonstrated control to your installed version. |

| CapCut | [CapCut Help Center](https://www.capcut.com/help) | Find the relevant help topic, checking whether it applies to Desktop, Mobile or Web. |

## Optional tutorials and deeper study

- [Casey Faris: Introduction to DaVinci Resolve](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MCDVcQIA3UM). Its chapter list covers the Media, Edit, Cut, Fusion, Color, Fairlight and Deliver pages. Open the chapter for the job in front of you.
- [Ripple Training: Learn Final Cut Pro in Under 60 Minutes](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HqUP7Zgeuck). A beginner introduction; check current controls against Apple's guide when your version differs.
- [Ripple Training: Final Cut Pro 12 Deep Dive](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=soIqczyU02w). A feature-specific reference for that version, including beat detection and transcript search. It is optional reading, not a reason to change your editor.
- [Cullen Kelly's resources](https://cullenkellycolor.com/). A starting point for further color-grading study. Check the chosen resource's format and any price before using it.
- [Walter Murch: In the Blink of an Eye](https://www.silmanjamespress.com/shop/filmmaking-directing/in-the-blink-of-an-eye2nd-edition/) and [Karen Pearlman: Cutting Rhythms](https://www.routledge.com/Cutting-Rhythms-Creative-Film-Editing/Pearlman/p/book/9781041024088). Optional books for deeper study of editing decisions and rhythm.
- [Netflix's Timed Text Style Guide](https://partnerhelp.netflixstudios.com/hc/en-us/articles/215758617-Timed-Text-Style-Guide-General-Requirements). A reference for Netflix deliveries; its requirements are not universal rules for every social video.

These links identify resources and their published topics. They are not a
guarantee that every technique fits your software, footage or style. Resource
pages and published video descriptions were checked on October 14, 2026;
the videos were not all watched end to end for this link review.

## Use one technique on a real edit

1. Choose a specific problem, such as an awkward transition or unreadable caption.
2. Give the AI the relevant link and timestamp. Have it check version-specific steps against current official documentation and your installed app.
3. Try the technique on a suitable section, then watch and listen to the result before keeping it.
4. Save only a useful recurring instruction in your existing private editing profile, with its example and the situation where it applies.

For a visual technique, the AI needs to inspect the example itself. A transcript
can explain steps, but it cannot show movement or whether a cut feels right.
Ask whether the AI watched the video, checked selected moments or only read
the transcript. [Lesson 10](../../Kit%20Email%20Workflow/lessons/10.md) explains
this approach.

For detailed starting values and their limits, use
[Editing Craft Numbers](EDITING%20CRAFT%20NUMBERS.md) and
[Sound and Color Numbers](SOUND%20AND%20COLOR%20NUMBERS.md). The recording,
current brief and actual result determine which advice is useful.
