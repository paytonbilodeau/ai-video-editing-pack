# Learning Path

Courses and sources worth an assistant's or an editor's time, chosen on
October 3, 2026 from public YouTube metadata (upload date, views, duration,
caption availability) and the creator's credentials. View counts are as of that
date and rounded. Views find a course; they do not prove its steps still match
your version, so check version-specific steps against current documentation
before acting on them. Give the assistant the transcript or the relevant
section when you want a technique adopted (see lesson 10 of the email guide).

## How to use a course with the assistant

1. Pick one technique for the edit in front of you, not the whole course.
2. Have the assistant check the app-version-specific steps against the current
   official documentation and your installed version.
3. Have the AI check the technique on a suitable section; then save a narrow reusable
   instruction in your private editing profile with the condition it applies to.

## Craft beyond any one editor

- Walter Murch, *In the Blink of an Eye* (the Rule of Six and the blink theory
  of the invisible cut).
- Karen Pearlman, *Cutting Rhythms* (tension and release; which shot, where,
  how long).
- YouTube's own creator help on audience retention, end screens and Shorts.
- Netflix Timed Text Style Guides and the BBC Subtitle Guidelines for caption
  timing floors.
- Curtis Judd (Learn Light and Sound) on dialogue leveling before compression,
  shotgun versus pencil mics indoors, and matching a lav to a boom.
- Cullen Kelly (Mixing Light, Frame.io) on color management and image
  formation; Jarle Leirpoll's Premiere color correction guide; Juan Melara on
  node order.
- Jenny Hoyos (Creator Science podcast) on shorts structure and retention.
