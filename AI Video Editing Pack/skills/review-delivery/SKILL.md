# Review, Delivery and Learning

Use [DELIVERY AND LEARNING](../../templates/DELIVERY%20AND%20LEARNING.md).
Save an editable project and export the intended version. Keep a clean master
when later reframing needs the final cuts without added graphics. The clean
master still contains original recorded visuals. Reopen the project and
confirm media is online. Decode the exports and check sync and complete ending.
Then watch and listen to each exact file being delivered, or clearly record
which intervals remain unreviewed.

Deliver the export, editable project and a short report of changes, route,
unverified items and suggested review points. Do not require a reply to mark
the requested editing work delivered. Invite corrections when the person is ready.
Do not substitute an AI summary, transcript, sampled frames, or technical
probe for human perceptual review. Preserve the accepted timeline before
applying changes. When the person adjusts it, compare the returned project
with the previous version: record changed source ranges and the reason, not
downstream timeline shifts caused by one cut.

Save a clear reusable preference from the person's instructions, with a
before/after example and a case where it may not apply. Put durable rules in
a private, versioned [editing profile](../../templates/EDITING%20PROFILE.md)
that the next project can read. Check each rule during later suitable work without assigning a separate test.
Saving instructions is not model training or proof of future quality. Use
[waterfall](../waterfall/SKILL.md) when derivatives were requested. Full-pass
mode does not require a separate approval of the source edit first.
