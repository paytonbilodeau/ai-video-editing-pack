# Color and LUT

Read the camera and source notes in
[PROJECT INTAKE](../../templates/PROJECT%20INTAKE.md). Identify mixed cameras,
lighting changes, log footage, baked-in looks, and any source whose color
interpretation is uncertain. Check the editor's color management for this
project instead of assuming a LUT or transform is appropriate.
Use the [motion and LUT guide](../../references/MOTION%20AND%20LUTS.md) if a
reference look or generated `.cube` is requested, and the color half of
[SOUND AND COLOR NUMBERS](../../references/SOUND%20AND%20COLOR%20NUMBERS.md)
for the order of operations, scope targets and per-editor panel order.

Correct exposure, white balance, and shot-to-shot skin and background
consistency first. Use scopes and actual frames together. If trying a LUT,
confirm that it is permitted, suited to the footage and applied at the right
stage; compare it off and on at matched viewing conditions. Remove it if it
crushes detail, shifts skin, or makes neighboring shots inconsistent. Keep
grades editable and retain an ungraded or prior version for comparison.

Review the clean export on the intended display path and record remaining
uncertainty in [EDIT PLAN AND REVIEW](../../templates/EDIT%20PLAN%20AND%20REVIEW.md).
Route a specific visual need to [style and motion](../style-motion/SKILL.md);
otherwise proceed to [review and delivery](../review-delivery/SKILL.md).
