# Lesson 14

Subject: Make the last few cuts yourself

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

Sometimes it's quicker to make a tiny cut yourself than explain it, wait for the AI and review another render. That's fine. You're allowed to touch your own video.

Ask the AI which exact timeline it worked on, open that timeline, and duplicate it before your finishing pass. A name like tutorial-final-touches makes the version easy to find later.

Illustration: Duplicate the timeline and make the last few creative touches yourself.

Learn three controls: split at the playhead, trim a clip's start to the playhead, and trim its end to the playhead. The playhead marks the frame you're looking at. Split divides a clip there; trimming moves one of its boundaries.

A ripple trim removes part of a clip and closes the resulting gap. It's handy for tightening a pause, but check the targeted tracks and linked audio so you don't move the picture without its sound.

In DaVinci Resolve, open Keyboard Customization and check the shortcuts assigned to those actions. Custom keymaps change those shortcuts, so check yours instead of copying someone else's keys. In another editor, ask the AI to find the equivalent controls for your version.

You only need a few familiar controls to remove a leftover mistake, tighten a pause or restore a word the rough cut clipped. Make the change on the duplicate, then play the cut at normal speed with sound.

Keep the earlier timeline. Tomorrow we'll compare your finishing pass with the AI's version and turn a useful correction into better instructions for the next edit.
