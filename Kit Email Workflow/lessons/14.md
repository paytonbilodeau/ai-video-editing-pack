# Lesson 14

Subject: When one quick cut is easier to make yourself

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

Sometimes it's quicker to make a tiny cut yourself than explain it, wait for the AI and review another render. That's fine. You're allowed to touch your own video.

If you want to make a finishing pass yourself, open the exact timeline the AI used and duplicate it. A name like tutorial-final-touches makes that version easy to find later.

Illustration: Duplicate the timeline and make the last few creative touches yourself.

Three controls cover many small fixes: split at the playhead, trim a clip’s start to the playhead, and trim its end to the playhead. The **playhead** marks the frame you’re looking at. Split divides a clip there; trimming moves one of its boundaries.

A ripple trim removes part of a clip and closes the resulting gap. It's handy for tightening a pause, but check the targeted tracks and linked audio so you don't move the picture without its sound.

The pack’s [Resolve](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/EDITOR%20PLAYBOOK%20RESOLVE.md), [Premiere](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/EDITOR%20PLAYBOOK%20PREMIERE.md), [Final Cut](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/EDITOR%20PLAYBOOK%20FINAL%20CUT.md) and [CapCut](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/EDITOR%20PLAYBOOK%20CAPCUT.md) guides cover their controls. Check the active keyboard preset: custom shortcuts can differ from the defaults.

Those controls can remove a leftover mistake, tighten a pause or restore a clipped word. Play the changed join with sound. If you’d rather describe the fix and let the AI do it, that works too.

Keep the earlier timeline. Comparing the versions lets the AI see exactly what you changed, which is useful material for your editing profile.
