# Lesson 5

Subject: The boring first pass can be automatic

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

Sometimes I don't need the AI to make creative decisions yet. I want it to remove obvious dead space so I can start from a cleaner recording.

The pack includes an [optional local pre-edit route](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/skills/pre-edit/SKILL.md) for that. It points to my free pre-edit tool, which runs on your computer. Some options need additional local software or a model download, so have your AI check the requirements first.

Illustration: Review a proposed pre-edit plan on a copied clip before generating a first pass.

Ask your AI:

Check whether the pack's local pre-edit route fits this recording. Show me a dry plan before changing anything: identify the exact source, the sections you propose removing, the reason for each removal and the output location. Explain which detection method you would use and what it can miss. Work from a copy and wait for my approval before rendering the first pass.

A dry plan shows what the tool intends to do without applying the edit. Look at the proposed removals before you approve them. A quiet word, a thoughtful pause and a genuine mistake can all look like low-volume audio, so aggressive silence removal can damage an otherwise good recording.

The result is a flattened MP4. You can watch it or import it into an editor, but it doesn't preserve every cut as a separate editable clip. Keep the source and the removal plan if you want to revisit a decision.

If your editor workflow already produces a good rough cut, skip this step. I'd rather get the video finished than add another tool because it exists.
