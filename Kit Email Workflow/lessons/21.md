# Lesson 21

Subject: Did AI actually save time on this edit?

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

The AI making a rough cut quickly is promising. What I care about is how long it takes to get a video I'd publish.

Count the setup, the first pass, your review, the revisions and the final export. If the AI saves twenty minutes cutting and creates forty minutes of repair work, we have something to fix.

Illustration: Compare total time, cost, and finished quality with your normal edit before claiming AI saves time.

Compare a finished AI-assisted edit with your usual process for a similar video. Keep the quality standard the same. A rough draft and a finished video aren't a fair comparison.

Include any extra costs the workflow required. If you're using an AI subscription you already pay for, distinguish that existing bill from new spending caused by this edit. If you can't estimate the time reliably, say that instead of inventing a precise saving.

A note like “The rough cut helped, but caption repairs ate the saving” is enough to identify what needs attention. If you did time the work, use those numbers. There’s no need to invent a precise saving or turn editing into bookkeeping.

The answer might be that rough cuts worked well but captions needed more direction. Great. Keep the rough-cut workflow and improve the caption instructions. You don't need to declare the whole setup brilliant or useless.

My own tests are why I'm excited about AI editing. Your finished videos will tell you which parts earn a place in your process.
