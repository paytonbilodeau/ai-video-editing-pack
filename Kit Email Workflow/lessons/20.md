# Lesson 20

Subject: Your next edit starts before you record

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

Some of the biggest editing wins happen before you record. A mic closer to your mouth, a quieter room and a screen recording people can read give the AI much better material to work with.

AI can help clean up a recording. It can't reliably recover the explanation you never recorded or the detail that's already blown out of the picture.

Illustration: Make a short recording test and improve the source before the full recording.

Before your next video, record ten seconds using the camera, mic and software you'll use for the real take. Say a few sentences and show a typical screen action.

Listen with headphones and look at the picture. Is the voice clear? Does the room echo? Is your face exposed properly? Can you read the text in the demonstration? If you're making a vertical video, is there room for the captions?

Ask the AI to inspect that sample and suggest one change you can make with your current setup. If it can't watch or listen to the file, describe what you noticed instead. Move the mic, change the light or enlarge the screen content, then record the test again.

Compare the two samples. Keep the change if it helps. You don't need a new shopping list every time a recording sounds a bit rough.

I want the AI spending its time on cuts, pacing and useful graphics. If we can fix the source in a few minutes, I'd rather do that than keep asking it to rescue the same recording.

Try that quick test before your next take. Your future self, currently staring at a timeline, will appreciate it.
