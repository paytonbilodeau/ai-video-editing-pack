# 09 · Describe the visual style

[All 22 lessons](<../SEQUENCE.md>) · [Open START HERE](<../../START HERE.md>)

Reading time: about 2 min.

When I made [the Netflix editing-style video](<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-qpMzjmSBU>), I didn't start with “make it look cool.” I collected reference images, described the movement and sound, and tested a small piece of footage. Then I kept changing the result until it felt right for my videos.

The pack includes my starting style recipes and editable motion tools. Choose an example close to your point, keep its useful structure, and adapt the words, colors and footage. A new custom look can wait until it helps a particular video.

A specific effect gives the AI something useful to aim at: a label that follows an object, a title that appears handwritten, or a diagram that builds as you explain it. The best choice makes your point easier to understand.

![Translate a reference into one specific visual behavior and test it on your own footage.](<../assets/lesson-09.jpg>)

Put a few clear examples in a reference folder and say what you like about each. For motion, include a video the AI can access and the exact timestamps. A still shows the design. The video shows how it enters, how long it stays and how it leaves.

Use a brief like this:

> Study these references for the effect I described. Explain its movement, timing, placement, lettering and sound. Suggest an original version using my own footage, words and brand assets.
> 
> 
> 
> Build a short editable test over the actual shot. Show the entrance, the time it stays on screen and the exit. Keep important screen actions and faces visible.

Be specific about the feel. “The label should follow the mug, settle gently and stay readable while I explain it” gives the AI a much better target than “add some cinematic motion.”

Judge motion with the dialogue and moving footage together. A beautiful animation on an empty background can cover your face, block the demonstration or compete with the point once it lands in the real video.

The [motion guide](<../../AI Video Editing Pack/references/MOTION AND LUTS.md>) explains how to describe a reference. You can explore a small sample first, or give the AI an established style to use throughout the edit.

For the included Tech Motion, Gentlemen or layered Sketch look, use the [matching source and render guide](<../../Payton Editing System/motion/flow/README.md>). Reproduce that example first, then change one detail. Neutral engine starters are separate checks.
