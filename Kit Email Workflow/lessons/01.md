# 01 · Your AI editing pack is here

Reading time: about 3 min.

[**Download your free AI Video Editing Pack.**](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/releases/latest/download/ai-video-editing-pack.zip)

You describe the video you want, and your AI works through the edit using the tools on your computer. Then you watch the result and tell it what to improve. I'm calling that vibe editing.

The aim is to hand over more of the work while keeping your taste in the finished video.

![Your first edit starts with footage, a practical instruction pack, an AI rough cut, and your own finishing judgment.](../assets/lesson-01.jpg)

Start with these three steps. You don't need footage ready yet:

1. **Download and unzip the pack.** A ZIP is a compressed folder. Double-click it on a Mac, or right-click and choose Extract All on Windows. GitHub hosts the download; you don't need an account or coding experience.
2. **Open the extracted pack in your local AI assistant.** In Codex, start a Local task in a dedicated editing folder. Select the extracted Vibe Editors folder or give its exact location, then ask the AI to list a few files it can read. Keep the pack together and your footage outside it. [The setup guide](../../AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/AI%20APP%20SETUP.md) covers Codex and Claude Code.
3. **Paste this message.** The assistant will check your existing tools and help you choose how much to hand over.

> Set up this Vibe Editors folder so you can be my video editor. Read its START HERE guide and follow the setup skill. Ask how hands-on I want to be: careful, balanced, or handle it for me. Reuse my existing tools, permissions, skills and preferences. Keep my originals and projects safe, and keep my private editing profile outside the pack. Explain any new access, upload, installation or cost before using it unless already authorized. Offer useful optional tools, then set up what fits. List a few files you can read in this pack, show where my private editing profile is saved, and separate editor actions you tested from actions that still need me. Tell me the simplest next step. No footage is needed for setup.

The download includes the editing skills, pre-edit and motion tools, and [all 22 illustrated lessons](../../Kit%20Email%20Workflow/SEQUENCE.md). You can read ahead and start editing now; the remaining emails walk through the same process one step at a time.

Share which editor you use and what you want to make in [Vibe Editors](https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors) if you would like help. The free pack and guide work independently.
