# Lesson 1

Subject: Your AI editing pack is here (let's use it)

Delay: 0 hours after confirmation

[Here's your free AI Video Editing Pack.](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/releases/latest/download/ai-video-editing-pack.zip) GitHub is where I keep the files. You don't need an account, coding experience or a GitHub tutorial to download them.

The pack gives your AI instructions for editing your own footage: cuts, pacing, audio, color, motion graphics, animations and repurposing. You explain what you want, review its work and add your final touches. I'm calling that vibe editing.

The goal is a useful first pass that you can review and finish. I want to help you reach that point with a workflow you understand, rather than promise a result for every recording.

Illustration: Your first edit starts with footage, a practical instruction pack, an AI rough cut, and your own finishing judgment.

1. **Download and unzip the pack.** A ZIP is a compressed folder. On a Mac, double-click it. On Windows, right-click it and choose Extract All.
2. **Open START HERE.md.** It's a text guide, not an app to install. Read it on [GitHub](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/START%20HERE.md) or open the downloaded file in a text editor.
3. **Give the pack to your AI.** Use a local assistant such as Codex that can work with files on your computer. Give it the unzipped folder or the guide link in the message below. A browser chat can help plan, but controlling your editor needs a separate connection.

Copy this message into your AI:

>
> https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/START%20HERE.md
>
> Ask about my computer, AI app, editor, footage and intended result. Check file access and editor control separately. Guide me through a 20- to 60-second test in a copied project. Keep originals intact, ask before installations or expanded access, and explain how I can check the result. If you cannot read the link, ask for the unzipped pack.

I'll send you one short lesson each day for the next 21 days. Each covers one part of the workflow, with an example or a prompt you can use. You already have the whole pack, so experienced editors can jump ahead. If you're new, start with the short test. An entire documentary can wait until tomorrow.
