# 01 · Your AI editing pack is here

Reading time: about 4 min.

[**Here's your free AI Video Editing Pack.**](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/releases/latest/download/ai-video-editing-pack.zip) GitHub is where I keep the files. You don't need an account, coding experience or a GitHub tutorial to download them.

The pack gives your AI the instructions, tools and templates for cuts, pacing, audio, color, graphics and repurposing. You describe the video you want; it works through the edit using the tools connected on your computer and returns a version you can watch and refine. I’m calling that vibe editing.

In my testing, AI has gotten an edit about 95% to 98% of the way there. That's my experience with my setup, not a promise for every recording. The aim is an editor that handles more of the work and gets closer to your taste as you use it.

![Your first edit starts with footage, a practical instruction pack, an AI rough cut, and your own finishing judgment.](../assets/lesson-01.jpg)

1. **Download and unzip the complete pack.** A ZIP is a compressed folder. Double-click it on a Mac; on Windows, right-click and choose Extract All. Keep the extracted Vibe Editors folder together.
2. **Open that folder in your local AI assistant.** Codex or Claude Code can work with files and run editing tools on your computer. [The setup guide](../../AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/AI%20APP%20SETUP.md) explains the app, plan and permission choices.
3. **Paste the message below.** Your AI checks what you already use, asks how hands-on you want to be, and creates or reuses your private editing profile. No footage needed yet.

Copy this message into your AI:

> Set up this Vibe Editors folder so you can be my video editor. Read
> AI Video Editing Pack/SKILL.md and its vibe-editing-setup skill first.
>
> Start by finding out how hands-on I want to be: careful, balanced, or handle
> it for me. Explain the relevant permission choices in my AI app and adapt
> to my answers. Reuse permissions and decisions I've already given you.
>
> Inspect my existing editor, tools, skills and workflow in the folder I share.
> Keep what works. Create or reuse my private editing profile outside the pack;
> preserve my preferences and save where to find it for future edits.
>
> Offer the included pre-edit tools and JavaScript, Remotion and HyperFrames
> motion options early. Explain what each adds, recommend what fits, and set up
> the options I choose. Inspect first; install only missing components through
> their official sources after any needed authorization. Don't make me type
> commands you can run. Help with Computer Use if available and wanted.
>
> Use short, clear explanations. Ask only for missing information that changes
> the next step. Keep originals and current projects recoverable. Explain
> specific new access, uploads or costs before using them unless already
> authorized. Respect the app's permission controls.
>
> Verify the available route. Finish with what works, what remains manual,
> where my profile lives, and how to give you my first video. Setup alone is
> not a request to edit. When I request an edit, complete the agreed work
> without routine stage approvals unless I've asked for them.

If the AI cannot see the folder, use its folder picker or give it the folder’s location. [The START HERE guide](../../AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/START%20HERE.md) can also help it explain the next step if you get stuck.
