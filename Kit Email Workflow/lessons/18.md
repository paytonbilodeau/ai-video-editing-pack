# Lesson 18

Subject: The MP4 isn't your project

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

An MP4 is the finished video you watch or upload. The editable project is where you can still change the cuts, audio, titles and effects. You want both.

Importing the finished MP4 into a new timeline won't restore those separate pieces. A caption baked into the picture stays part of the picture.

Illustration: The editable project and the finished MP4 are different outputs; keep both.

Before the final export, ask your AI to save a clearly named version of the approved project and confirm where its media and graphics live.

> Save a clearly named version of the approved editable project. Reopen it and confirm that clips, audio, titles and effects load. Tell me which source folders, fonts and graphics it depends on.
>
> Export a separate MP4 for my intended platform using the approved timeline and current requirements. Give the exact project and export locations, with distinct filenames for horizontal and vertical versions.

Keep the project and the files it depends on together in a folder structure you understand. If you move or delete the original media, the project may open with missing clips even though the MP4 still plays.

Keep a pre-service master if you use Auphonic or another final audio service. It gives you a clean starting point for revisions and shorts, so the same voice isn't enhanced twice.

Open the exported MP4 outside your editor. Check that you exported the right timeline and that the picture, sound and captions made it into the file.

If you're making a vertical version too, give it its own project or timeline name and export filename. Tutorial-horizontal-v1.mp4 and tutorial-vertical-v1.mp4 are much easier to recognize than final-final-new-2.mp4.
