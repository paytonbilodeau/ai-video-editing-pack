# Lesson 16

Subject: One video can give you a very good short

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

A good short should make sense to someone who never watched the longer video. Pulling out the loudest sentence isn't enough if the viewer needs three minutes of context to understand it.

Start with an approved long-form edit and ask the AI to find a few possible clips. Have it explain why each one works before it starts cutting.

Illustration: Extract one complete idea from a longer edit, including its setup and payoff.

Find three possible shorts in this approved video. Give me the exact source timestamps, the idea each clip teaches, and the setup and payoff it needs. Keep qualifications that affect the meaning. Don't edit yet. Recommend the strongest option and explain why it would make sense to someone seeing me for the first time.

Watch the strongest candidate from beginning to end. If it starts with “this is why it works,” can you tell what “this” refers to? If it promises a result, does it show or explain that result before the clip ends?

Once the idea works on its own, ask the AI to build that one short. Then refine the opening, vertical framing, pacing and captions for the platform you're posting on.

I'd rather publish one useful clip than export twenty and hope a few are good. You can still record a separate short when the idea needs a different setup. Repurposing is useful, but it doesn't make every section of a long video worth posting twice.

Pick one complete idea from your current edit and turn it into one short you would happily put your name on.
