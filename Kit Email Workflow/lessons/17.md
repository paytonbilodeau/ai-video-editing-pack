# Lesson 17

Subject: Your vertical crop can hide the whole point

Delay: 1 day after the previous lesson

Your video can look great on a laptop and lose its whole point in a vertical crop. Your face is centered, but the button you're explaining is somewhere outside the frame. Excellent tutorial.

Automatic reframing gives you a starting point. You still need to check what the viewer has to see: the subject, the captions and the part of the demonstration that makes the explanation useful.

Illustration: Keep the important face and demonstration clear of mobile app controls.

Name the destination when you ask for a vertical version. Your AI should duplicate the timeline and check the framing across the whole clip, especially whenever the screen content changes.

A **safe area** is the part of the frame where essential content stays visible around a platform’s controls. Your AI should check current guidance; the platform’s preview, when available, shows how its buttons and caption area sit over the video.

Sometimes a face above a screen recording works better than a tight crop. Sometimes you need to enlarge one part of the demonstration. Choose the layout that helps someone follow the video.

If you’re unsure about a new layout, ask for a short preview before the rest is rendered. That’s a convenient way to compare options, not a required step for every vertical video.
