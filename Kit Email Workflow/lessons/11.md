# 11 · You can prompt the motion graphics too

Reading time: about 2 min.

You can describe a motion graphic and have AI build it without writing any code yourself. Then you can refine it in normal language, just as you would the rest of the edit.

Motion graphics are optional. If your footage and explanation already make the point, keep working toward the finished export.

Start with what the viewer needs to understand. “Show these three steps as I explain them” gives the AI a useful job and a reason for each part of the animation.

![Keep the editable motion-graphics source, render a video clip, then place it in your editor.](../assets/lesson-11.jpg)

For a simple title or arrow, your video editor may already have everything needed. A keyframe records a setting at one moment. Give a title one position at the start and another a second later, and the editor creates the movement between them.

More involved graphics can use [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev/docs), [HyperFrames](https://hyperframes.heygen.com/quickstart), or the included [JavaScript animation kit](../../AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/skills/javascript-animation/GUIDE.md). These let the AI build animations with code. JavaScript Canvas acts like a drawing surface; Remotion can also include that animation in a larger project. You describe the result, and the AI handles the code.

The [motion setup guide](../../AI%20Video%20Editing%20Pack/references/MOTION%20TOOL%20SETUP.md) helps it choose and prepare a suitable tool. You don't need every option installed for one graphic.

Have it test the animation over the actual footage, with the dialogue playing. Check the entrance, the time it stays visible, and the exit. If it sits over your video, ask for a transparent background in a format your editor supports; an ordinary MP4 won't provide transparency.

Once the movement works, add sound only if it helps. A click should line up with the visible click or landing, so trim any silence at the beginning of the sound file or move it earlier. Watch the exported result with sound to check the timing.

Keep the editable source, fonts and assets together. That way, changing three steps to four won't mean rebuilding the graphic from scratch.
