# Your visual editing walkthrough

[Open or download the large infographics](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WmqVZb6UrvRVzjxaZlif9KwEDHSNiP-K)

Anyone with the link can view.

Follow one video from setup to a checked export. The pictures lead; copy a prompt when you need the next action. Replace bracketed fields with your own details. The free pack works independently, and [the full 22-lesson guide](../SEQUENCE.md) adds detail when needed.

The drawings are illustrative examples, not app screenshots or proof of a finished edit. They use a lamp switch to connect the steps.

[Open or download the lamp practice ZIP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1fhak1vH4Px1Zwk7k2KiNuflmx7yYFAnB/view). It includes the source, an intentional mistake and the corrected edit. The media use synthetic images and a generated click, with no filmed lamp or human voice. Start with [lesson 3](03.md#try-the-lamp-practice).
