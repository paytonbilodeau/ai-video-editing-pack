# 07 · Export and Check Your Video

[Open or download the large infographics](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WmqVZb6UrvRVzjxaZlif9KwEDHSNiP-K)

Anyone with the link can view.

Reading time: about 3 min.

[Keep the project](../lessons/18.md) · [Final watch](../lessons/19.md)

Keep the source, the editable project and the finished video. Each has a different job, and an export alone cannot preserve all your editing choices.

## Keep the work editable

![Source media, the editable project and the exported video have different jobs and should all remain available.](assets/07-project-and-export.png)

Save the project with access to its source media, captions and graphic files. Reopen it before assuming the package is complete.

```text
Save the editable project and identify its source media and supporting files. Reopen it and report any missing media, fonts, graphics or unsupported effects.
```

## Export the reviewed version

![Keep the editable project with its source media, then play the actual final video and check sound and captions on the intended screen.](assets/07-delivery.png)

Give the export a clear filename. The lamp example should retain the click, the visible result and readable captions.

```text
Export the reviewed timeline for [destination] as [filename]. Keep the project and source unchanged, and tell me the exact path of the exported file.
```

## Watch that actual file

![Watch the entire exact exported file, checking sound, captions, picture and the ending, including the phone view.](assets/07-final-check.png)

Play the whole export at normal speed with sound, including the ending. Check the intended screen size. If the AI cannot play it, do that review yourself.

```text
Check the exact export’s picture, sound, sync, captions and ending. Say which checks you actually performed. If a correction is needed, fix the project, export again and recheck the new file.
```

Use an optional final audio service once, after visual changes. Keep the pre-service version and check the returned file’s sound and sync.
