Cut what gets in the way while keeping the point understandable. These diagrams show the idea; watch and listen to your own joins.

## Try the lamp practice

[Open or download the lamp practice ZIP](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1fhak1vH4Px1Zwk7k2KiNuflmx7yYFAnB/view), unzip it, and open START HERE.md. Play source.mp4, too-short.mp4 and corrected.mp4 in that order with sound. The mistake removes the useful click; the correction keeps it and time to see the result. The media are synthetic, with no filmed lamp or human voice.

## Keep the action

![An illustrative lamp edit removes an unnecessary wait while retaining the complete instruction, switch click and visible result.](assets/03-cut.png)

In the illustrated lamp edit, the wait can shrink while the click and visible result remain. Quiet time can still be useful.

```text
Work on a duplicate. Remove the unused wait, keep the switch click with the action, and leave enough time to see the result. Preserve the original.
```

## Keep complete speech

![A gap is shortened while the speech on both sides remains complete; the new join still needs listening.](assets/03-keep-complete-words.png)

If your recording has speech, keep complete words and thoughts. The synthetic lamp practice has no recorded speech.

```text
Play across each changed join with sound. Restore any clipped word, missing action sound or pause the viewer needs to follow the demonstration.
```

## Keep cuts adjustable

![Video, voice and text remain separate editable layers instead of only a finished video file.](assets/03-editable-layers.png)

Use an editable timeline when you want to move individual cuts later. The optional pre-edit tool instead returns a first-pass video and a cut report.

```text
Use the editable route supported by my editor. Show what remains adjustable, and verify the imported timeline before calling it ready.
```

Use spoken restart markers only after agreeing what they mean. The optional pre-edit guide explains them.
