# 02 · Describe your video

[Open the brief image](<assets/02-profile-vs-brief.png>) · [Copy the editing brief](<../lessons/03.md>)

 

Reading time: about 2 min.

A brief tells the AI what to do with this recording. Your editing profile holds preferences you reuse across videos.

![The lamp demo brief keeps the switch click, complete sentence and readable caption; the profile holds preferences for future videos.](<assets/02-brief.png>)

### 1. Describe this video

The lamp brief keeps the click and visible result while cutting unused waiting. Replace the brackets below for your own video.

> Edit [exact recording or path] for [audience] on [platform], in [horizontal or vertical] format. Read my existing editing profile. Show [main point], keep [important material] and remove [known mistakes]. Preserve complete explanations, the opening’s setup and payoff, and time to follow each action. Work on a duplicate, editable timeline and keep the original intact. Return the project, export and anything I should review. Reuse existing preferences and ask only for missing details that change the result.

![Reusable preferences from a saved profile combine with the brief for this particular video.](<assets/02-profile-vs-brief.png>)

### 2. Reuse your preferences

Keep this video’s choices in its brief. Reuse your saved profile for recurring choices. If setup copied Payton’s starting preferences, adjust that copy after a small preview; an existing custom profile takes priority.

![Specific reference choices for pacing, color and readable text combine into an editing style.](<assets/02-style-recipe.png>)

### 3. Add a reference if it helps

Show the moment you like and say why. For movement, include a video timestamp. Ask for a short preview when trying an unfamiliar look.
