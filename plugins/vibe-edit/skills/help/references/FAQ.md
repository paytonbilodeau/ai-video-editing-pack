## 2. What is the pack, and what does it actually do?

[Install Vibe Edit](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/INSTALL.md) · [Plugin overview](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/README.md)

The pack is now a plugin called Vibe Edit: editing instructions, tools, examples and all 22 illustrated lessons, installed into Codex or Claude Code, which can work with files on your computer.

Three pieces work together: the AI assistant carries out your request, the plugin guides its work, and your video editor holds the timeline. Setup checks which connection works with your existing tools.

Describe the video you want, let the AI make the edit, then watch and refine it. Keep the editable project as well as the finished video. The free plugin works independently; community help is optional.

## 4. What computer and software do I need?

[Check your AI setup](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/setup/references/AI%20APP%20SETUP.md) · [Compare editor connections](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/editors/references/EDITOR%20CONNECTIONS.md)

Start with the equipment and subscriptions you already have. Let the AI check the operating system, editor edition and version, available connection, and whether your recording opens.

The walkthrough uses a Mac and Resolve. Other editors and Windows have different routes, so test your actual setup before buying anything. Keep originals recoverable and personal work outside the pack.

## 6. Which AI plan should I pay for?

[ChatGPT Plus](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6950777-what-is-chatgpt-plus) · [ChatGPT Pro tiers](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9793128-about-chatgpt-pro-tiers) · [Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)

Start with an eligible plan you already have. If you're choosing a first paid plan, ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro gives you a lower-cost way to try the workflow before committing to a higher allowance.

At the October 12, 2026 check, ChatGPT Plus and monthly Claude Pro were listed at US$20 per month. ChatGPT Pro and Claude Max have options starting at US$100 per month.

Prices, taxes, access, and allowances can change, so check the provider's current page before paying.

A higher plan becomes worth considering when real editing work repeatedly hits the usage limit, or you need a particular available model or feature. It doesn't guarantee better creative choices or unlimited editing. The same recording can require very different amounts of AI work depending on the brief, tools, and number of revisions.

Use one representative video to learn where your current plan becomes inconvenient. Record the interruptions and extra charges, if any. That gives you a reason to upgrade that is based on your work.

API or extra-usage billing should be checked separately before enabling it.

## 7. How does the cost compare with hiring an editor?

[Source: Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/hire/video-editors/)

The comparison depends on what you currently pay an editor to do and how much of that work you want to handle yourself.

Upwork currently describes video-editor rates of roughly US$10–$60 per hour. Its examples include US$200–$600 for a batch of three to five simple social clips and US$500–$1,500 for a 10–20-minute YouTube package that includes additional deliverables. Those are marketplace examples, not a universal rate card or a quote for your video.

For a simple comparison, a US$100 monthly AI plan equals two hours at US$50 per hour. That arithmetic doesn't mean the plan replaces two hours of professional work.

You still provide direction, review the edit, and handle whatever the workflow can't do reliably.

Count all of your costs: AI subscription, editor license, optional tools, community access if purchased, and your own time. The pack itself remains free. Software is separate from membership.

AI is attractive when it helps you produce edits you are happy to publish at a lower total cost or with more control. An experienced human editor may remain the better choice when you want someone else to own the creative decisions and delivery.

## 10. Can I keep using Final Cut Pro?

[Final Cut playbook](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/editors/references/EDITOR%20PLAYBOOK%20FINAL%20CUT.md)

Yes. Final Cut can receive a supported timeline file called FCPXML. It describes an editable arrangement of clips; it is not a rendered movie and still needs the original footage.

The AI can prepare that handoff, while some controls or export steps may need Computer Use or your click. Test a separate project, then check cuts, linked sound, fonts and media before relying on the route.

Keep the project and source files together. If something fails, include your Final Cut version and the exact step in Questions & Chat.

## 13. Why can't my AI see the pack or my footage?

[Setup skill](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/setup/SKILL.md)

First check the plugin is loaded: in Claude Code the skills appear as /vibe-edit:setup and so on; in Codex they are offered by name in a new session. If they are missing, the install did not finish; the install page lists the exact commands.

For footage, ask the AI to name a few files in the exact folder you supplied. That checks whether it can actually read them.

List a few files you can read in [exact folder location]. If you cannot read them, tell me what is missing and the next step in this app. Do not claim the editor is connected just because the files are readable.

If it cannot, check that the right folder is selected, external drives are connected, and cloud files have downloaded completely. A visible cloud placeholder may not contain the file yet.

Use the assistant's folder picker or give the exact location. A project folder organizes work; it does not restrict an assistant that already has broader permissions.

If it remains blocked, share the app, operating system and error in Questions & Chat. Keep private account details out of the post.

## 14. What is an editor connection, and which permissions should I allow?

[Permissions explained](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/setup/references/AI%20APP%20SETUP.md) · [Safety and API keys](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/setup/references/SAFETY%20AND%20API%20KEYS.md)

File access lets the AI read or change files. An editor connection lets it perform supported actions in the video editor. Computer Use lets it see and operate the screen. These are separate capabilities.

Your working preference, such as “handle it for me,” tells the AI how much to carry through. The app's permissions determine what it can access.

Have it explain the specific permission, purpose and setting before granting new access. Reuse decisions already made, keep originals recoverable, and treat an API key like a password. Downloaded instructions cannot give permission on your behalf.

## 15. How do I make the AI edit in my style?

[Editing profile template](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/edit/templates/BLANK%20EDITING%20PROFILE.md) · [Style lesson](https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors/classroom/9370c6bd?md=eb6b685ff408447e9193e1848345c222&utm_campaign=skool_link_post&utm_content=42041b66d6e64317a930b9e046188b34)

Show a reference and say what you like about a specific moment: its pacing, captions, color or movement. Say what you do not want copied too.

Let the AI test that idea on your footage. A short preview helps when the look is uncertain; otherwise it can finish the agreed edit and take feedback afterward.

Ask it to save useful recurring choices in your private profile with an example and when they apply. Keep a one-video exception in that video's brief. The next edit shows whether the instruction helped.

## 16. What should I give the AI for my first edit?

[First-edit brief](https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors/classroom/9370c6bd?md=22a321f1fb5044d4bf4c31af91b98ff4&utm_campaign=skool_link_post&utm_content=42041b66d6e64317a930b9e046188b34)

Give the AI the exact recording, your existing profile and a short brief. Name the audience, platform, video shape and main point. Include any important material to keep, known mistakes to remove, and assets or references it needs.

For example:

Edit this recording for YouTube using my profile. Keep the complete explanation and the opening's setup and payoff. Remove false starts and dead space, while leaving time to see the screen actions. Work on a duplicate and return the editable project, export and anything I should review.

Ask for a preview if you are choosing a new style. A routine edit can continue through the work you authorized.

## 17. The AI cut the wrong take or made the pacing awkward. What should I do?

[Revision-note prompt](https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors/classroom/9370c6bd?md=d42df605046845a7ae5be1f8621cb4df&utm_campaign=skool_link_post&utm_content=42041b66d6e64317a930b9e046188b34)

Keep the version you reviewed. Give its timestamp, what happened and what you want changed: “At 01:16, the first sound of the next word is missing. Restore the complete word.” For a wrong take, identify the correct source phrase or time.

Ask the AI to preserve the rest and replay the changed section with a few seconds on each side. A transcript can locate the words; the recording shows whether the cut sounds natural.

Dictate notes if that is faster than typing. Save a preference only when its reason should apply to future edits.

## 18. Why does my audio sound rough, quiet, or out of sync?

[Audio guide](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/edit/references/core/AUDIO%20GUIDE.md)

Listen to the same short section in the source, timeline and export. That helps locate where the problem began.

Have the AI choose the intended microphone track, avoid duplicate dialogue and diagnose the actual hiss, rumble, distortion or timing problem. Compare processing at similar volume. Use headphones for small differences and an ordinary speaker for clear speech.

Optional Auphonic finishing comes after cuts, color, captions and graphics, once per finished export. It offers a free allowance and paid additional use. Keep the pre-service version for revisions and shorts, and preserve deliberate untreated examples in audio demonstrations.

## 19. Can the AI make captions, images, and motion graphics?

[Motion Graphics course](https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors/classroom/cc00277c) · [Motion setup](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/style/references/MOTION%20TOOL%20SETUP.md) · [Safe areas](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/edit/references/core/REFRAMING.md)

Yes, when the available tools support the result. You describe what the viewer needs to understand; the AI can handle the code. Start with the editor for a simple title or pointer, and add a motion tool when it helps.

Test the graphic over the actual footage with dialogue. Give it time to be seen, understood and read. For vertical shorts, keep essential content clear of controls at the top, right and bottom while keeping the composition centered.

Check caption spelling, fonts, asset rights and any transparent overlay in the final export. Keep the editable source for revisions.

For captions, generate them from the final cut or import a subtitle file and align it. Correct names and numbers against the recording, check each line at normal speed, and keep the text clear of the demonstration. Keep an editable copy and choose captions built into the video or a separate subtitle file for your destination.

## 20. My export is wrong or the project says media is offline. What now?

[Export and project checks](https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors/classroom/9370c6bd?md=a86457cdf9d84b16a93f49de44e93c4c&utm_campaign=skool_link_post&utm_content=42041b66d6e64317a930b9e046188b34)

Keep the project and source files while diagnosing the problem. “Media offline” means the editor cannot find a file it expects. Check the drive, filename and cloud download, then use the editor's relink control with the correct source.

For a bad export, compare the exact timeline and file at the same moment. Give the AI the filename, timestamp, problem and editor version.

Fix the project, export a new version and watch the changed section with sound. Reopen the project too. Keep both the editable project and final video so another correction does not mean starting over.

## 22. How do I update the pack without losing my preferences?

[Update skill](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/blob/main/plugins/vibe-edit/skills/update/SKILL.md) · [Release notes](https://github.com/paytonbilodeau/ai-video-editing-pack/releases)

Your private profile, footage and projects are never inside the plugin, so an update never touches them.

In Claude Code run `/plugin marketplace update vibe-editors`. In Codex run `codex plugin marketplace upgrade`, then start a new session. If you installed from a downloaded folder, replace the folder. Then say "Update Vibe Edit to the latest version and keep my profile."

The update skill confirms the new version, re-tests only what changed and keeps your preferences and custom files. If you still have an older folder install (a `Vibe Editor Pack` folder in your workspace), the same skill moves you across without deleting anything.

If something fails, keep the error and ask for help with the version and step. The version label alone does not prove the update worked; a small test does.
