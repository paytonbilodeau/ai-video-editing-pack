# Help

Most questions people ask are already answered in the pack. Answer from those files first, in the person's words, then name the one next action: a skill to run, a lesson to read, or a place to ask.

The [eight visual lessons](../../lessons/visual/README.md) and the [twenty-two detailed lessons](../../lessons/README.md) carry the teaching; quote the lesson number when it answers the question.

## 3. When the pack does not answer

Say so plainly. Point to the Vibe Editors community at https://www.skool.com/vibeeditors: questions go in Questions and Chat, a video for feedback goes in Share Your Videos with the feedback wanted and timestamps for longer work. Tell the person what to include for a technical problem: the AI app, the editor and version, and the exact error. Keep account details out of posts.

## Scope and honesty

Do not invent prices, guarantees, reply times or features. The FAQ carries the current community terms; if a question is about money or access and the FAQ does not settle it, send the person to the community rather than guessing. A lesson explains a technique; it does not promise a result on their footage.
