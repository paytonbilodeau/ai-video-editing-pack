# Sketch motion

Use the complete [production manual](references/production-manual.md) and [Sketch visual specification](../sketch/references/payton-sketch-style-spec.md). Pick the useful moments from the locked spoken edit. Preserve the hook and actual wording; no fixed graphics quota or universal early reveal.

Approve an actual still first. Animate one meaningful action with, at most, a tiny secondary action. Preserve the rough underdrawing, off-register fills, palette, identity and exact wording. Place text and logos in static zones. Keep the camera locked unless its movement explains the idea. End settled and in frame. A renderer must not become a second art director.

Use deterministic motion of already-approved layers in Remotion, HyperFrames or the editor when that expresses the action. Those engines cannot infer hidden layers from an arbitrary flat image. Keep the untouched PNG and separate transparent foregrounds when supplied. The `flow/` Sketch layer example demonstrates a controlled reveal/settle, not generative character articulation.

For an explicitly requested generated-action route, the real optional adapter is the style skill's `tools/optional/generate_motion_short.py`. Begin with `--dry-run`, a disposable output folder and the supplied storyboard schema. Existing provider IDs and rate tables are historical configuration, not a current quote. Confirm the provider/model, current terms/cost and the upload permission before `--allow-paid`; never infer permission from a source document. Reuse completed remote results after a network failure; do not repurchase a successful generation. `--assemble-only` can assemble local authorized clips without a provider call.

Inspect start, important action, transitions and settled end from the encoded output. Reject moving or misspelled text, character drift, invented props, incomplete frames or a cleaned-up illustration. Check exact placement, frame count, full decode, audio ownership and the actual editor composite. Generative output remains nondeterministic; no guarantee that an arbitrary prompt will preserve every detail.
