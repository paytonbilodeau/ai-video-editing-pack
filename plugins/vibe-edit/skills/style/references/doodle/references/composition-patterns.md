# Composition and original metaphors

Choose one primary structure:

| Structure | Useful for | Physical staging |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workflow | Input, processing, output | Materials enter, Xiaohei transforms them, a result emerges; sparse orange movement |
| System detail | Sources, filters, storage, agents | Three to five meaningful physical parts; Xiaohei operates a critical one |
| Before and after | Disorder to order, manual to automatic | Two states joined by one action |
| Character states | Overload, blockage, recovery | Two to four small states with one short label each |
| Concept metaphor | An abstract causal idea | One unusual object or machine, few inputs, one output |
| Method layers | Frameworks and capabilities | Stacked physical objects that Xiaohei builds or moves; no formal pyramid |
| Route | Learning, handoff, idea to launch | A winding path with few stops and a working character |
| Small comic | A process or change | Two to four scenes, each with one action |

Invent the metaphor from this subject:

1. Convert the abstraction to a physical action such as sticking, leaking, sorting, settling, opening, folding, unpacking, or returning.
2. Choose one or two low-tech objects, such as a drawer, pipe, letterbox, scale, ladder, gate, spool, clothesline, or strange workstation.
3. Make Xiaohei perform the essential action. Oddness must clarify the relationship rather than merely entertain.

Examples are for line density, whitespace, restrained color, and character temperament. Do not copy their staging unless the user asks. Avoid automatically repeating the bundled conveyor breakpoints, content judgment lever, sorting funnel, material fish, handoff rope, layered sources, megaphone/bridge/door trio, stamped toolbox, or warning-sign path. The same topic can use a new object and action.
