# Preserve the object that carries the thought

A stable background alone does not create continuity. Keep the same document
while its content changes, scroll its content out before showing a footer, or
retain a signed page while annotations arrive. Choose a different operation
when the meaning changes: a network, path, evidence crop or comparison.

Remove permanent labels that do not help the current idea. Keep short contextual
source captions when needed. Give important phrases hierarchy and attach emphasis
to their actual ink. Do not fill a hold with movement just because it is still.

Fade or move a replaced heading fully clear before the next enters its space.
Preserve checklist state through its exit; do not reset values during travel.
Inspect intermediate positions as well as final poses. A dissolve between two
copies is not the same as moving one retained object.
