# Choose geometry from meaning

| Relationship | Useful operation |
| --- | --- |
| Name changes, object does not | Retain the object and change its nameplate |
| A broad field narrows to one task | Select an item and transform the same item |
| A word has two meanings | Retain the root while branches reveal at their cues |
| One idea is contained by another | Expand a shared enclosure around it |
| A source qualifies a claim | Establish the real page, then crop to its excerpt |

Keep one timeline owner for an object that crosses a scene boundary. Interpolate
its bounds and anchors; clear old labels before geometry crosses them. Preserve
the transforming object, not every annotation of its old state. A justified
comparison may still use two columns. Variety is not a quota.

Inspect the sequence as small thumbnails with the words mentally removed. If
unrelated ideas repeat the same layout and behavior, revisit their mechanisms.
Then inspect native detail. An all-frame thumbnail atlas can show complete
temporal coverage at its recorded size; it cannot prove full-resolution text
inspection or acoustic listening.

Budget entrance, settled reading time and exit separately. Use the actual pause
before the next phrase where appropriate. Do not retime approved narration to
satisfy an unverified criticism of pace.
