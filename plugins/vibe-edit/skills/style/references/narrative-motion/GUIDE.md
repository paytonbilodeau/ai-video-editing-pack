# Narrative Motion

Make the explanation happen on screen. A document can stay visible as it becomes
a summary; a selected item can move into a larger process. Preserve the object
that connects the thoughts instead of resetting to a new title card each time.
Narrative Motion is a separate choice from Editorial, Tech Motion and Gentlemen.
The technical folder name remains `seamless-editorial` for compatibility.

Start with the existing customer profile, requested stage and exact locked edit.
Use real word timing from that audio. A synthetic fixture must label its cues
as invented. Do not change accepted cuts, audio, captions or another selected
style to fit a new graphic. An optional presenter must fit the real composition.

1. Map the spoken relationship: a change, comparison, process, branch, container
   or authentic source inspection. Choose geometry before choosing a card.
2. Record the initial state, meaningful change, retained object, source/rights,
   cue, settled reading interval and exit. Do not invent statistics or proof.
3. Use a warm light stage, charcoal type, serif headings and restrained green
   as starting choices. The customer's palette wins. Keep permanent counters,
   category labels and ornamental rails out unless useful or requested.
4. Animate local changes first. Preserve one object's identity across related
   sentences. Use a directional wipe at a real idea change, starting around
   one-third of a second and adapting it to the actual frame rate and reading.
5. Separate entrance, settled reading time and exit. A correctly timed word can
   still disappear too quickly. Let a genuine pause carry the completed idea.
6. Preserve authentic screenshot pixels. Crop into the relevant evidence and
   annotate outside it. Illustration is not a captured live interface or proof
   of a successful action. Keep identity and archival context correct.
7. Render settled frames and consecutive boundary frames, then inspect the
   complete requested duration. Check glyphs, shared anchors, source crops,
   entrances, holds and exits. Numerical checks and sampled frames do not prove
   continuous watching, listening, user acceptance or business outcomes.
