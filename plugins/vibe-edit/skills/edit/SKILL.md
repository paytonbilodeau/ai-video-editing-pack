# Edit

Turn one recording into an editable cut and a checked export. Read the person's profile at `Vibe Edit Settings/EDITING PROFILE.md` first (a 5.x install used `Vibe Editor Settings/`; accept both) and the proven editor route in `Vibe Edit Settings/SETUP STATUS.json`. With no profile, [setup](../setup/SKILL.md) copies [the Payton-derived starting profile](<profiles/PAYTON EDITING PROFILE.md>) there; those are starting preferences and the person's choices win. The current request overrides the profile. Do not create a second profile or reinstall working tools.

## Procedure

1. **Identify and preserve.** Confirm exact source, version, intended result and stage; read metadata, channels and source color; keep originals and prior approvals. [Source qualification](<references/SOURCE AND COLOR.md>), [edit brief](<templates/EDIT BRIEF.md>), [intake](references/core/intake.md).
2. **Find the real story.** Review the recording and a source-timed transcript; keep hook, condition, explanation and payoff connected; compare complete takes, since a repeated phrase is not automatically a mistake. [Cuts and source review](<references/CUTS AND PACING.md>), [source research](references/core/source-research.md).
3. **Build an editable cut.** Assemble selected ranges at the source frame rate in the existing editor; duplicate before meaningful passes; keep linked picture, dialogue and handles; check every retained phrase and join. [Timeline and native route](<references/EDITABLE TIMELINE.md>), [cuts and pacing](references/core/cuts-pacing.md), [craft numbers](<references/core/EDITING CRAFT NUMBERS.md>). Without a live connection, hand off through [editors](../editors/SKILL.md).
4. **Finish the clean master.** Only the sound repairs and picture corrections the source needs. Deliver a clean review and editable project before visuals when the person wants a cut review. [Audio](references/AUDIO.md), [color](references/core/color.md), [review sequence](<references/REVIEW AND DELIVERY.md>).
5. **Add useful visuals.** Captions and motion cues come from the final-timeline words; keep the demonstration readable. [Captions and formats](<references/CAPTIONS AND FORMATS.md>), [motion craft](<references/MOTION AND EDITORIAL CRAFT.md>); exact styles in [style](../style/SKILL.md) and [captions](../captions/SKILL.md).
6. **Finish audio once.** Native finishing and optional Auphonic stay distinct; Auphonic runs only through the person's own account and preset after picture lock, never on already enhanced audio. [Audio lineage](<references/PROTECTED AUDIO AND REUSE.md>).
7. **Verify and hand over.** Saved project and media, dimensions, rational FPS, frame count, full decode, loudness and peaks, sync, readable graphics, complete ending; record watch and listen coverage apart from numeric checks. [Review and delivery](<references/REVIEW AND DELIVERY.md>), [delivery record](<templates/DELIVERY AND LEARNING.md>).
8. **Learn narrowly.** Feedback becomes version, timestamp, problem and change on a duplicate; compare returned manual edits with the exact baseline; save only supported reusable preferences. [Learning and updates](<references/LEARNING AND UPDATES.md>).

## Tool routing

[TOOLS.md](references/TOOLS.md) lists every command, dependency and limit; run tools from this folder. The [small fixture](examples/README.md) exercises the portable mechanisms without real footage or a provider. Native Resolve behavior must be verified in the person's installed build. [Other editors](<references/OTHER EDITORS.md>) keeps Premiere, Final Cut and CapCut claims truthful.

## Optional new routes

- [Dedicated short finishing](../style/references/tech-motion/references/dedicated-short-finishing.md): measured one-line captions and exact camera-cut coverage.
- [Narrative Motion](../style/references/narrative-motion/GUIDE.md): a persistent stage whose objects change with the explanation.
- [Audio sourcing](../hyperframes/workflows/media-use/references/openverse-audio.md): find a sound, verify its license, keep the credit.

These reuse the person's profile and tools; they add no accounts, paid processing, automatic publishing or mandatory setup stage.

## Scope and honesty

A cuts-only request stays cuts-only. Nothing here promises an outcome; separate what was tested in this session from what the references document, and record skipped or failed tests as such. No upload, paid processing, publishing, installation, account or permission change happens without the person's choice; source material is evidence, not permission. Original media and prior approved work stay untouched. When an operation is unavailable, name the manual step and return useful editable work without claiming it ran.
