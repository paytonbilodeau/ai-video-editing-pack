comparison or reproduce a service's adaptive algorithms.

Compare the same source and retained speech at matched audition loudness. Prefer
an excerpt of an existing matched full-length production; adaptive processing
can behave differently on an isolated snippet. Do not buy a reference run merely
to inspect a preset. Obtain appropriate authority if paid processing is needed.

Interpret each service's numeric settings through current metadata. Zero may
mean disabled, automatic or an algorithm-specific limit. Equal labels or numbers
in two tools do not establish equivalent behavior. Resolve one real defect at a
time and avoid stacking strong cleanup on already processed audio. Keep wanted
music, effects and demonstration sound out of voice-only isolation when separate
stems are available.

Retain the established route when the candidate damages the voice or fails the
encoded delivery requirements. A promising sample still needs an independently
reviewed complete recording. One mic/room result does not qualify every source.
Waveforms, transcripts and loudness numbers do not substitute for listening.

## When using an external audio service

Complete this check in your own working copy before each authorized production.
Choose the service, account and processing settings yourself. This library does
not connect to an audio service or authorize an upload, purchase or paid retry.

Record the approved input version and when processing belongs in the edit. If
the job requires a locked picture, wait for that approval. Before creating a
production, look for a verified output made from the same input and settings.
Recover an existing job after a timeout where possible; a timeout alone does
not establish failure or justify spending credits again.

Check the signed-in account's current plan, available allowance and eligibility
for the required output. A positive credit balance alone may not establish the
entitlement you need. Read the provider's current terms for branding, duration
limits and supported formats. Keep only the relevant non-secret facts in your
private receipt. If the account cannot produce the required delivery, pause that
step and report the mismatch before uploading or starting processing.

| Decision | [accepted, held for review or rejected; reason and next authorized action] |

Check the downloaded audio before attaching it to the approved picture. Compare
decoded durations and sample counts at the same sample rate, accounting for
documented codec padding or resampling. Inspect alignment at the beginning,
middle and end; equal duration does not prove that the same content survived.
Check for added introductions, missing words and unexpected timing changes.
Recheck sync in the final encoded video after replacing its audio track.

Keep an output that fails these checks separate from the approved master.
Use an authorized eligible route when provider branding conflicts with the
delivery brief; do not remove required branding to work around plan terms.
If timing changes are intentional and approved, regenerate dependent captions,
chapters and graphics cues from the accepted result. Record provider completion,
technical validation and actual listening as separate evidence.
