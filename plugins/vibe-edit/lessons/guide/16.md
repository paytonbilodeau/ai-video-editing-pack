# 16 · One video can give you a very good short

Reading time: about 2 min.

The pack works for videos recorded as shorts as well as longer videos. You can give it a vertical recording and follow the same editing process from the start.

If you already have a longer video, you also have another option: turn one complete idea from it into a short. That's the [Content Waterfall System](../../skills/shorts/SKILL.md). The longer video is the source, and useful smaller pieces flow from it.

If you only need the original video, skip repurposing and continue with its final review and export.

![Extract one complete idea from a longer edit, including its setup and payoff.](assets/lesson-16.jpg)

A good short still needs to make sense to someone who never watched the original. If it begins with “this is why it works,” the viewer needs to know what “this” means. Keep enough setup to understand the point and enough of the ending to deliver it.

Ask your AI:

> Make one strong short from this video for my chosen platform. Choose a complete idea with enough setup and a clear payoff. Preserve any qualification that changes the meaning. Use my editing profile, keep the project editable, and check framing, captions and sound. If no section works on its own, tell me rather than forcing a clip.

You can request candidates first if you'd like to choose the moment. Otherwise, let the AI make the agreed short and explain its choice when it delivers the project, source ranges and export.

If you use a final audio service, make the short from the version saved before that service. Finish the short, then process its own final export once. This avoids enhancing already processed speech again.

A strong short can also become the idea for a longer video, but that usually needs new recording and a fuller explanation. Repurposing can't recover material you never recorded.

I'd rather have one useful short than twenty clips that need the original video to make sense. Pick the formats that serve your content; you don't have to make every possible version.
