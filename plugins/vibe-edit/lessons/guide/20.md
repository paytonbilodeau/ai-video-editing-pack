# 20 · Your next edit starts before you record

Reading time: about 2 min.

After finishing an edit, notice which problems started in the recording. Fixing one of those before the next take can save another round of cleanup.

A closer microphone, quieter room or more readable screen recording gives the AI better material. It can improve a recording, but it can't reliably recover an explanation you never recorded or detail that was lost in a bright, overexposed shot.

![Moving the microphone closer can give the editor a clearer voice recording.](assets/lesson-20.jpg)

When you change your camera, microphone or room, record a short test with a few spoken sentences and the screen action you'll demonstrate. Play back the saved file, rather than judging only the live preview. Check that the voice is clear, the sound matches your mouth, and the viewer can read the controls.

If something needs work, ask your AI to help using the equipment you already own. Moving the mic or enlarging screen text may solve more than another filter. Have it say what it actually watched or listened to, so you know what its advice is based on.

For vertical video, leave enough room around your face and the important action to adjust the framing later. If you use restart markers, agree on their exact phrases and meanings before recording, then say them clearly when you restart.

Use the test to check the change and then record. The aim is a better starting file, not a new shopping list every time something sounds rough.
