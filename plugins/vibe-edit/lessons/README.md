# Your complete illustrated editing guide

**[8-step visual walkthrough](visual/README.md)**

All 22 lessons are here from day one, with their illustrations and copyable prompts. Read from the beginning or jump to the part you need. The daily emails are reminders; they do not unlock parts of the plugin.

For setup, open [the plugin README](../README.md) or give your assistant [INSTALL](../INSTALL.md). The software used for editing and rendering is installed separately when needed.

## Finish your first edit

The shortest route is a clear brief, a usable cut, the corrections your footage needs, and a checked export. Use these lessons as you reach each step:

1. [Set up your assistant and editor](guide/01.md), [choose its permissions](guide/02.md), then [write the brief](guide/03.md).
2. [Check complete words and useful pauses](guide/04.md).
3. [Keep speech natural](guide/06.md) and [correct the picture where needed](guide/07.md).
4. [Check captions and any graphics you use](guide/12.md), then [give precise corrections](guide/13.md).
5. [Keep the editable project](guide/18.md), [watch the actual export](guide/19.md), and [save useful preferences](guide/15.md).

The automatic pre-edit tool, creative LUTs, custom graphics and repurposed shorts are optional. Add one when it helps the video in front of you. Use the full index below when you need another technique.
