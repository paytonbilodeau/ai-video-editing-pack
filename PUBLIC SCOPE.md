# Public scope

Only four projects belong in the public catalog: AI Video Editing Pack, Today in AI, AI Mentorship and Echo Improvement. Editing-related tools and the email funnel are parts of the editing offer.

Do not add the personal memory vault, assistant setup, private agent infrastructure, general operating systems, private style recipes, customer records or personal configuration. A source change is a reason to review a public workflow, never permission to export a private system. Rewrite a reusable mechanism, inspect the diff, run validation and read back the public release.

The newsletter repositories teach the process and provide editable templates. They do not grant permission to publish to an author's accounts or include their private artwork recipe.
